Will Linux work on your PC?

GreyHaven checks the parts inside your PC, tells you which version of Linux fits best, then walks you through switching, one step at a time. It won't change anything without asking.

Download for Windows

Free and open source · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · No account, no ads

How it works

  1. 1Check

    Scan your PC in under a minute. Every part is checked against Linux's own driver lists, so you see what works, what needs one extra step, and which apps and games come with you.

  2. 2Choose

    Pick Ubuntu, Linux Mint or Pop!_OS, with plain pros and cons. Keep Windows too, so you can pick at every start, or replace it.

  3. 3Switch

    GreyHaven downloads Linux, checks it's the official file, makes your USB stick, and puts the next steps on your phone with a QR code. Then it restarts your PC into the stick.

Is it safe?

It asks before it changes anything

Scanning needs no administrator rights and changes nothing. Before GreyHaven changes a Windows setting or writes your USB stick, it says exactly what will happen, then Windows asks your permission, for that one thing only.

It only erases what you choose

Only USB drives are ever listed, never the drive Windows is on. You tick a box naming the stick before anything is erased, and big external drives need a second tick so your backup is safe.

It checks every download

Linux comes from the official source over a secure connection. GreyHaven checks the file's fingerprint and, for Ubuntu and Linux Mint, their digital signature, then reads the stick back to make sure it was written right.

Your details stay on your PC

GreyHaven never reads your files, and your app list never leaves your PC. Online checks send part ID numbers only, and you can turn them off. Privacy policy

Keep Windows, and you can go back

You try Linux from the USB stick first, without installing anything. Not happy? Take the stick out and Windows starts as before.

Open source

Every line of code is on GitHub under the MIT licence, and every download has published fingerprints so you can check it came from there.

Questions

Will I lose my files?

Not if you choose Keep Windows too: Windows, your apps and your files stay as they are, and Linux gets space next to them. GreyHaven still asks you to back up first, because changing the drive's layout is the one step that can go wrong. Replace Windows erases the drive, and GreyHaven won't let you go on until your backup checklist is done.

What do I need?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC (64-bit), a USB stick of 8 GB or more that can be erased, an internet connection for the download (about 6 GB), and your phone for the guide. Allow an hour or two.

Which versions of Linux?

Ubuntu 26.04 LTS, Linux Mint 22.3 and Pop!_OS 24.04: three of the most popular, all free and well supported. GreyHaven suggests the one that fits your PC and explains why.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that normal?

For now, yes. GreyHaven is new, and Windows warns about apps it hasn't seen many people download yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You can check the file is the real one with the fingerprints in SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.

Does it send anything about me?

Only if you leave online checks on: the ID numbers of parts GreyHaven doesn't recognise go to linux-hardware.org, and it asks GitHub whether there's a newer version. No names, no files, no app list. See the privacy policy.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no account, no paid version. It's an open-source project.
